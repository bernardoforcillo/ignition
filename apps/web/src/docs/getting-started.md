# Getting started

Using Ignition takes three steps: create an account, confirm your email address and create a workspace.

## Sign up

1. Open the sign-up page and enter your email address and a password.
2. Choose a password that meets the [password policy](/docs/authentication#password-policy). The form shows the rule and checks it before sending anything.
3. Submit the form. You will see a "Check your email" message.

Signing up with an address that already has an account looks exactly the same on screen. If the address is taken, the owner receives an email saying so instead of a verification link. This keeps the page from revealing who has an account.

## Verify your email

Open the message we sent and follow its link. The link carries a single-use token and opens the web app, which confirms your address. Until you do, you cannot sign in.

A link that has expired or been used already is reported as invalid, and you cannot sign in until the address is verified.

## Create a workspace

After your first sign-in you land on the onboarding screen:

1. Enter a workspace name. A URL-friendly slug is suggested from it, and you can change it.
2. Create the workspace. Slugs are unique, so a name that is already taken asks you to try another.

You become the owner of the workspace, and it starts on the Free plan. From there you can [invite teammates](/docs/workspaces#inviting-people) or look at [plans and billing](/docs/billing).

## Joining someone else's workspace

If a teammate invites you, follow the link in the invitation email. Signed out, you are asked to sign in first and then returned to accept the invitation. You can belong to several workspaces and switch between them from the app.
